Effective November 21, 2026

These terms apply when you claim a business listing on near.by, post deals, or pay to pin a deal. By doing so you agree to them.

1. Your listing and deals

  • Listing your business and posting deals is free.
  • You must be the owner or an authorized manager of the business you claim.
  • Deals must be real, available as described and honored at the times shown. Keep them up to date and pause them when they end.
  • Deals must follow the law, including Utah alcohol laws. Don't advertise prohibited alcohol promotions.
  • We may edit, pause or remove deals or listings that are inaccurate, reported or break these terms.

2. Pinned (sponsored) deals

  • A pinned deal appears first in the near.by feed, labeled "Sponsored", for people within the area you choose around your business, on the days you choose.
  • Price = the rate per mile of area (measured across) per day × miles × days, shown before you pay. The standard rate is $10 per mile per day.
  • Founding businesses: the first 50 businesses in each city to use pinned deals get the founding rate ($5 per mile per day) for 12 months from their first pin.
  • Your first pin is a free trial of up to 7 days.
  • Each person sees one pinned deal at a time. Where several businesses pin the same area, they take turns, weighted by what each pays per day. At most three pins can overlap one location.
  • A pin only shows on days its deal runs and while the deal is active. If you pause the deal or the pin, it stops showing; paused days are not refunded.
  • View and tap counts in your dashboard are estimates. We don't guarantee a number of views, customers or sales.

3. Payment and refunds

  • Payments are processed by Stripe on its secure checkout page. A pin starts once payment goes through.
  • You can cancel a pin before it starts for a full refund by emailing us. After a pin has started, refunds are at our discretion (for example if near.by had an outage).
  • If we remove a pinned deal for breaking these terms, we don't refund it.

4. Listings created from public information

To help people find local deals, near.by lists businesses and deals using publicly available information, such as business websites, public event calendars and other public sources. If you own a listed business, you can claim the listing to correct or manage it, or email us to correct or remove it, and we'll act promptly. If you believe content on near.by (such as a photo) infringes your rights, email us and we'll remove it.

5. Reps

If a near.by rep helped you, you can enter their rep code in your dashboard. The rep is paid by near.by from what you pay; it never costs you more. Reps are independent and can't change prices or terms on our behalf.

6. Other terms

near.by is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to what you paid us in the 3 months before a claim. We can change prices or these terms with 30 days' notice; changes don't affect pins you've already paid for. These terms are governed by the laws of Utah.
