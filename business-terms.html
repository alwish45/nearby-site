Business Terms

Effective November 27, 2026

These terms apply when you claim a business listing on near.by, post deals, or pay to promote a deal. By doing so you agree to them.

1. Your listing and deals

  • Listing your business and posting deals is free.
  • You must be the owner or an authorized manager of the business you claim.
  • Deals must be real, available as described and honored at the times shown. Keep them up to date and pause them when they end.
  • Deals must follow the law, including Utah alcohol laws. Don't advertise prohibited alcohol promotions.
  • We may edit, pause or remove deals or listings that are inaccurate, reported or break these terms.

2. Sponsored (promoted) deals

  • A promoted deal appears in near.by labeled "Sponsored" (on Home, the Map, the Calendar and in plan suggestions) for people within the area you choose around your business, on the days and at the times you choose, and, if you choose, only to people looking at the kinds of things you pick or who follow or saved similar places.
  • You set a daily budget and a number of days, and pay the budget × days up front (less any credit you have). We charge your budget per 1,000 views delivered, at the rate shown before you pay (standard $8 per 1,000 views). A view is counted once per phone per day. We never charge more than your daily budget in a day, or more than you paid in total.
  • Whatever isn't spent when a promotion ends or is cancelled becomes credit on your account, used first on your next promotion. Credit doesn't expire while your listing is active and isn't paid out in cash, except as described under Payment and refunds.
  • Founding businesses: the first 50 businesses in each city to promote get the founding rate ($4 per 1,000 views) for 12 months from their first promotion.
  • Your first promotion is free for up to 7 days, at up to $5 a day. Unused free budget doesn't become credit.
  • Any number of businesses can promote in the same area. Each person sees a limited number of sponsored posts (about one for every eight listings), shared among the businesses that match, weighted by what each has left to spend that day. We don't guarantee a number of views, customers or sales; you're only charged for views delivered.
  • Targeting by interests and similar places is worked out on each person's phone. We don't sell or share personal information about the people who see your deal, and we don't tell you who they are.
  • A promotion only shows while its deal is active and approved. If you pause it, or the deal is switched off or held for review, it stops showing and the days it misses are added back to the end. View and tap counts in your dashboard come from near.by's own records.

3. Payment and refunds

  • Payments are processed by Stripe on its secure checkout page. A promotion starts once payment goes through.
  • You can cancel a promotion before it starts for a full refund by emailing us. After it has started, what it hasn't spent comes back as credit; cash refunds are at our discretion (for example if near.by had an outage).
  • If we remove a promoted deal for breaking these terms, we don't refund it.

4. Listings created from public information

To help people find local deals, near.by lists businesses and deals using publicly available information, such as business websites, public event calendars and other public sources. If you own a listed business, you can claim the listing to correct or manage it, or email us to correct or remove it, and we'll act promptly. If you believe content on near.by (such as a photo) infringes your rights, email us and we'll remove it.

5. Reps

If a near.by rep helped you, you can enter their rep code in your dashboard. The rep is paid by near.by from what you pay; it never costs you more. Reps are independent and can't change prices or terms on our behalf.

6. Other terms

near.by is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to what you paid us in the 3 months before a claim. We can change prices or these terms with 30 days' notice; changes don't affect promotions you've already paid for. These terms are governed by the laws of Utah.
