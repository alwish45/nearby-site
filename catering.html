How it works

  1. Choose when, how many people, delivery or pickup, and any dietary needs.
  2. Pick a caterer and their packages. near.by suggests enough for your headcount.
  3. Send the order. The caterer confirms the final total (tax, tip, extras) and how you'll pay them: card, invoice or another way they offer.
  4. You get a notification when it's confirmed. Nothing is charged by near.by.

For caterers

Free to list. 10% when you confirm.

Put your packages on near.by (per person, per tray or per item, with minimums and dietary tags), your notice time, delivery area and minimum order. You confirm each order with the final price; near.by's fee is 10% of the confirmed total, at least $5, taken from your near.by credit. Declined or cancelled orders cost nothing.
