A promoted deal shows, marked "Sponsored", on Home, the Map, the Calendar and in plan suggestions, to people within the area you pick around your business. Choose when it shows (say weekdays 3–6pm) and who sees it (people looking at food, live music, trivia, family things… or fans of places like yours).   3. Post your happy hour or special.
