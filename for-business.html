Get started in 5 minutes

  1. Download near.by from the App Store.
  2. Go to the You tab → Claim your listing and sign in with your email.
  3. Post your happy hour or special.
  4. Tap 📌 Pin on a deal to start your free week.
  5. If a near.by rep helped you, enter their rep code in your dashboard.
