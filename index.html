How a plan comes together

1 · Propose itChoose a day and a few stops from the happy hours, specials and events nearby, or add your own: a picnic, a hike, games at your place.
2 · Invite your peopleSend one link by text or group chat. Friends join from their browser, no app or account needed.
3 · Shape it togetherEveryone ticks the times that work, votes on places and suggests others. Each time has its own thread for "can we do 10:30?"
4 · Go do itLock it in, see who's coming and get directions between stops. Want a text the morning of? Just add your number.
