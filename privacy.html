Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

near.by helps people along the Wasatch Front and in Park City find local deals, happy hours and events, lets business owners post and promote deals for their listing, and lets reps earn by bringing businesses on. This policy explains what information the near.by app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

WhatWhenWhy
Your device locationOnly if you allow it, while you use the appTo show deals near you. Your coordinates are sent with each search to find nearby deals. When the feed loads, we also save a rounded location (to about a third of a mile) with a random ID that changes every time you open the app, so we can tell businesses roughly how many people use near.by in an area. It isn't linked to you, your device or an account, and we don't keep a location history about you. If you don't allow location, the app shows deals around Park City, and you can search any area by hand.
Deal activityWhen you see a sponsored deal, open a deal, tap Directions or tap CallTo show businesses how many people saw or acted on their deals. We record the deal, the action and a random ID that changes every time you open the app. It isn't linked to you or your device.
Deal reportsWhen you report a deal as wrong or expiredTo fix or remove inaccurate deals. We record the deal and the reason you picked.
Email addressBusiness owners only, when signing inTo create and secure your business account and send sign-in codes.
Business and deal detailsBusiness owners only, when claiming a listing or posting dealsTo publish your listing and deals in the app.
Promotion purchasesBusiness owners only, when paying to pin a dealPayments are processed by Stripe on its own secure page. We receive confirmation that you paid, the amount and the email used. We never see or store your card number.
Rep informationOnly if you sign up as a near.by repYour name, email, rep code, the businesses linked to you and what you've earned, to credit and pay you. Tax details we need before paying you (such as a W-9) are collected outside the app and kept only as long as the law requires.

People who just browse deals don't need an account, and we don't ask for their name, email or contact details.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app contains no advertising SDKs.
  • We don't use your location in the background.

Service providers

We store data with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, which processes it on our behalf. Stripe processes payments for promotions. Maps are shown by Apple Maps. Opening directions, a phone call or a business website hands you off to those apps or sites, and their own privacy policies apply.

Keeping and deleting your information

Deal activity and reports are kept to provide analytics and keep deals accurate; rounded area pings are deleted after 90 days. Business owners and reps can delete their account at any time in the app (You → Delete account). That deletes the login, email address and rep profile and releases any claimed listing. We keep payment and payout records as long as tax and accounting laws require. The public listing and its deals may stay in the app, unclaimed. You can also ask us to delete your information using the contact below.

Children

near.by is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
