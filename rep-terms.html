Rep Terms

Effective November 26, 2026 (direct earnings 40%; recruiter bonus 25% of your recruits' earnings, for 24 months)

The short version. Joining is free. You earn a share of what the businesses you bring to near.by pay for promotions, for a limited time. You never pay us anything, you have no quotas, and you earn nothing just for recruiting people: only real business payments count.

These terms are between you and near.by ("we", "us") and apply when you sign up as a near.by rep in the app. By tapping "Become a rep" you agree to them.

1. What a rep does

Reps introduce local businesses to near.by and help them list their business, post deals and, if they choose, pay to pin (promote) a deal. You may also invite people to use the app and invite others to become reps.

2. Independent contractor

You are an independent contractor, not an employee, partner or agent of near.by. You choose when, where and how you work and you pay your own expenses. You can't sign contracts, offer discounts or make promises on our behalf beyond what the app and our published prices offer.

3. Getting credit for a business

  • A business is linked to you when its owner enters your rep code in their near.by dashboard, or when we confirm a claim you made in the rep portal after checking with the owner.
  • Each business can be linked to one rep at a time. If two reps claim the same business, the code the owner enters wins.
  • The link lasts 24 months from when it's confirmed. If the business makes no paid promotion within 90 days of being linked, the link lapses and another rep may earn credit.
  • You can't earn credit for a business you own or manage, or by entering your own code.

4. What you earn

  • Direct earnings: 40% of each payment a linked business makes for pinned deals while the link is active.
  • Recruiter bonus: if another rep signed up with your code, you earn 25% of what that rep earns in direct earnings from their linked businesses, for 24 months after they joined. This comes out of near.by's share. It never reduces what the other rep earns. There is only one level: you earn nothing from reps recruited by your recruits.
  • No self-dealing: you earn nothing on a business you own, manage or work for, or on a rep account that is yours or is run for you. Using a second account, or a friend's, to collect commission on your own spending is not allowed; we reverse those earnings and may close the accounts.
  • Free trials, discounts we fund, taxes and refunded or charged-back payments earn nothing. If a payment is refunded after you were paid, we subtract it from your next payouts.
  • We show your earnings in the rep portal. Our records are final unless you tell us about an error within 60 days.

5. Getting paid

  • Earnings are released 30 days after each business payment (the refund window).
  • We pay monthly once your released balance is $25 or more, by bank transfer or another method we agree on.
  • Before your first payout we'll ask for a W-9 (or other tax form). We'll send a 1099 when the law requires. You're responsible for your own taxes.

6. How to represent near.by

  • Be honest. Only describe features and prices that are in the app. Don't guarantee results, customers or sales.
  • Don't pressure businesses, misrepresent who you are, or use near.by's name in ways we haven't approved.
  • When you share near.by with people (social posts, texts, reviews), say that you're a paid near.by rep.
  • Follow anti-spam laws: don't send bulk texts or emails to people who haven't agreed to hear from you.
  • Respect businesses' wishes: if an owner says no, don't keep contacting them.

7. Ending

You can stop being a rep at any time by deleting your account or emailing us; we'll pay released earnings over $25. We can suspend or end your rep account, and withhold unpaid earnings tied to the problem, if you break these terms, act fraudulently or harm near.by's reputation. We can change rates or these terms with 30 days' notice in the app; changes don't reduce earnings on payments made before the change.

8. Other terms

near.by is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to the earnings we owe you. These terms are governed by the laws of Utah.
